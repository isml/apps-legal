Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Lowcort can read data from Apple Health (recorded by Apple Watch or other wearables), or you can use the 60-second camera scan: place your fingertip over the rear camera and flash, and hold still. You can also explore every feature in Demo mode (Settings → Demo mode).

The camera scan says the quality is too low.

  • Cover both the camera and the flash completely with the pad of your fingertip, pressing lightly.
  • Rest your hand on a table and stay still and quiet for the whole 60 seconds.
  • Warm hands work better. Remove a thick case if it blocks the flash.
  • Devices without a flash (such as some iPads) cannot scan; use Apple Health or Demo mode.

Does Lowcort measure cortisol?

No. The Stress Load is a cortisol-style estimate based on heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and respiratory rate. Cortisol itself can only be measured with a blood, saliva or urine test. Lowcort is not a medical device. See Settings → How Lowcort works.

My score says "No recent data".

Lowcort never guesses. If Apple Health has no recent measurements (for example your watch was charging), take a camera scan or check that Lowcort has Health access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Lowcort.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lowcort, or Lowcort → Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything Lowcort stores on your device and our server-side usage record. Mindful Minutes already saved to Apple Health remain in the Health app, where you can delete them. See our Privacy Policy.

What does the AI coach see?

Only after you tap Allow: a short summary (stress band, driver changes, tags, goal) and your message. No raw health data, name or location. You can turn it off in Settings.
