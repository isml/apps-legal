Lowcort: Cortisol Tracker for iPhone

Lowcort turns heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep into one clear number, your Stress Load (a cortisol-style estimate), and helps you lower it with short guided resets. Works with Apple Health or a 60-second camera scan. Lowcort is a wellness app, not a medical device.
