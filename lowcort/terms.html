Health and safety

Lowcort is a general wellness app. The Stress Load is a cortisol-style estimate derived from heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and respiratory rate; Lowcort does not measure cortisol or any hormone and is not a medical device. Camera scans estimate pulse and heart-rate variability only. Nothing in the app, including the AI coach, is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified health professional about any health concern. Stop any exercise that makes you feel unwell.

Not for emergencies. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call your local emergency number. In the US, call or text 988.
