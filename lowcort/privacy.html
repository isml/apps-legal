Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026 · Applies to the Lowcort iPhone app

In short: Your Apple Health data and camera scans stay on your iPhone. We do not sell data, we do not track you across apps, and you do not need an account. If you choose to use the AI coach, only a short summary (no raw health samples, no name, no location) is sent to generate a reply, and only after you allow it.

Who we are

Lowcort is developed and published by Ismail Karakas ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: ismailkappstore@gmail.com.

Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

  • With your permission, Lowcort reads heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate, sleep, respiratory rate, workouts and steps to estimate your daily Stress Load.
  • Lowcort writes only Mindful Minutes after you complete an exercise.
  • This data is processed and stored on your device. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to analytics, and never stored in iCloud by Lowcort.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings. Lowcort keeps working with the camera scan or Demo mode.

Camera scan

The 60-second fingertip scan uses the camera and flash to estimate pulse and heart-rate variability. Video frames are processed in memory on your device and are never saved or uploaded. Scan results are stored only on your device and are not written to Apple Health.

AI coach (optional, with your consent)

The coach and AI daily insight are off until you tap Allow on a consent screen. When allowed, the app sends this summary to our server (Google Cloud / Firebase), which forwards it to Google Gemini (Vertex AI) to generate a reply:

  • your Stress Load band and score, and the percentage change of its drivers (for example HRV, resting heart rate, sleep hours);
  • tags you selected (for example caffeine, alcohol), your chosen goal and your app language;
  • the message you typed and up to the last 6 messages of the conversation.

Raw health samples, your name and your location are never sent. Our server does not log prompts or replies. Google processes the request to generate the response under its Cloud terms and does not use it to train its models. You can turn the coach off at any time in Settings → AI coach; without consent, insights are generated on device.

If you report a response, we store only the message ID and a report category — not the content.

Service providers

  • RevenueCat
  • PostHog

Providers process data on our behalf, mainly in the United States.

Retention and deletion

  • Settings → Delete all data erases everything Lowcort stores on your device, deletes your AI usage record on our server and resets your analytics ID.
  • Mindful Minutes already saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can delete them yourself.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing and legal obligations.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data.

You can also email us to request access to or deletion of data held by our providers. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

Lowcort is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not a medical device

Lowcort is a general wellness app. Its Stress Load is a cortisol-style estimate; it does not measure cortisol or any hormone and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
